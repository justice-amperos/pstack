# Task record and reusable workflow brief

Keep this with the team's working documents, not in the installed skill. Fill only what the task needs; infer from existing context and mark unresolved choices. It is a compact handoff, not an intake form.

- Outcome and audience: what decision or ongoing responsibility this supports.
- Scope and evidence: sources, population, time window/timezone, exclusions, freshness expectations, and source-of-truth precedence where known.
- Working assumptions and forks: material defaults, what could invalidate them, alternatives tested, deciding evidence, and rollback or next check.
- Judgment: what warrants attention, accepted exceptions, examples of a clear finding and an ambiguous case. Mark proposed rules as provisional.
- Authority: allowed reads, drafts, shared edits and delivery destinations; actions that still need the owner. Scope and expiry of standing permission if provided.
- Finish and effort: useful deliverable, checks that establish its quality, investigation limit, and escalation conditions.
- Continuity: canonical artifact, state location, checkpoint/late-arrival strategy, recurring cadence and notification rule if requested. Scheduling is a separate authorized action.

## Run record

Record the actual coverage and gaps, rubric version, evidence pointers, findings and changes since the prior run, authorized actions and verified outcomes, and unresolved decisions with recommendations, held actions, independent work, and the exact next step. Retain stable finding identifiers and a last-successful checkpoint. A separate attempted-run timestamp must not disguise a coverage gap.

For each open finding, retain only enough to resume: identity, first/last observation, evidence, interpretation, status, next action, and an owner or review date only when established. A correction supersedes a prior interpretation without erasing its history.
